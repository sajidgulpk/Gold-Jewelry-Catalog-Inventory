1. Introduction

Welcome to Gold Jewelry Catalog & Inventory (the "App"). We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data. This Privacy Policy explains how our app collects, uses, and discloses information when you use our mobile application.

2. Information We Collect

  • Local Inventory & Catalog Data: All jewelry items, descriptions, weights, karats, and pricing entered into the app are stored locally on your device using local database storage (Room DB). We do not upload your personal inventory data to any external cloud servers.
  • Camera and Gallery Images: The app requests access to your camera and device gallery strictly so you can capture or select photos of jewelry pieces. These images are stored locally in your app's private directory and are never shared or transmitted by us.
  • Advertising (Google AdMob): We use Google AdMob to display ads in the app. Google AdMob may collect and use certain data, such as your device’s advertising ID, IP address, and interaction data with ads, to serve personalized or non-personalized advertisements.

3. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide and maintain the core functionality of the jewelry catalog, inventory, and gold rate calculator.
  • To display advertisements via Google AdMob to support the free usage of the app.
  • To improve app performance and user experience.

4. Google AdMob & Third-Party Advertising

Our app integrates Google AdMob as a third-party advertisement vendor. Google AdMob uses cookies and mobile advertising identifiers to serve ads based on your prior visits to our app or other apps on the internet.

  • Personalized vs. Non-Personalized Ads: Depending on your region and consent preferences (such as GDPR/EEA compliance), Google AdMob serves either personalized or non-personalized ads.
  • For more information on how Google uses data when you use our app, please visit Google’s Privacy & Terms policy.

5. Data Security

We value your trust in providing your inventory information, thus we are striving to use commercially acceptable means of protecting it. However, remember that no method of transmission over the internet or method of electronic storage is 100% secure and reliable.

6. Children’s Privacy

Our app does not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under the age of 13. If you are a parent or guardian and you are aware that your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so we can take necessary actions.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy
