Privacy Policy

Gold Jewelry Catalog & Inventory Management
Last Updated: September 2026

Privacy Commitment: Gold Jewelry Catalog & Inventory is designed with an Offline-First, Privacy-First philosophy. All your catalog items, customer invoices, custom gold rates, and product images are stored locally on your device. We do not collect, track, sell, or transmit your personal or business data to external servers.

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy outlines how Sajid AI Lab ("we", "our", or "developer"), operated by Sajid Khan, handles information when you use the Gold Jewelry Catalog & Inventory mobile application ("App").

By downloading, installing, or using this application, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this Privacy Policy.

2. Information Collection and Storage

Our application functions primarily as a local inventory, catalog, and valuation tool for jewelers and gold enthusiasts:

  • Local Database Storage: All inventory records, item names, weights (Tola, Gram, Ratti, Masha), purity levels (24K, 22K, 21K, 18K), making charges, stone deductions, and customer receipts are saved exclusively in a local SQLite database (via Room) on your Android device.
  • Zero Server Data Collection: We do not operate external databases or cloud analytics that collect your business data or inventory listings.
  • No Account Registration Required: You can use all core features of the App without creating an account or providing your name, email, or telephone number.

3. Device Permissions & Usage

The App requests only the minimal permissions required to provide essential features:

  • Photo & Media Access (Android Photo Picker): Used solely when you choose to attach product photos or shop logos to jewelry catalog items. We use the privacy-safe Android Photo Picker (PickVisualMedia) which provides zero broad storage permissions.
  • Camera (Optional): If you choose to take real-time photos of jewelry pieces directly from within the app to add them to your inventory.
  • Internet Access: Used exclusively to open developer support links (e.g., direct WhatsApp message, email inquiries) or to check optional online live gold rates if enabled by the user. No inventory data is uploaded or transmitted over the internet.

4. Data Security and User Control

Because your data resides on your physical Android device, you have complete and total ownership over your data:

  • Data Backup & Export: You can export your catalog data (CSV / JSON / PDF) or create encrypted database backups at your discretion.
  • Data Deletion: You can permanently wipe or delete individual items, clear categories, or delete all app data at any time through the in-app Settings menu or Android App Info settings.
  • Offline Functionality: The application functions completely offline without requiring an active internet connection.

5. Third-Party Services and Analytics

The App does not integrate third-party advertisement networks, tracking SDKs, or data broker tools. Standard system-level Google Play Services (such as basic crash reporting provided by the Android OS) may collect anonymous diagnostic data as governed by Google's Privacy Policy.

6. Children's Privacy

The App is a professional inventory and catalog management tool and does not knowingly collect or solicit personal information from children under the age of 13. If you believe that a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so that we can take necessary action.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy periodically to reflect app updates or regulatory compliance. Any changes will be posted within the application and on this page with an updated revision date.
